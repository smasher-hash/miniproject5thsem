🪲 Pest & Disease Information

1️⃣ Rice Blast Disease

Cause: Fungus (Magnaporthe oryzae)

Symptoms: Gray spots with brown margins on leaves.

Prevention: Use resistant varieties, avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer.

2️⃣ Cotton Bollworm

Cause: Insect larvae (Helicoverpa armigera)

Symptoms: Bores into cotton bolls and damages seeds.

Prevention: Use pheromone traps, neem oil spray, biological control.

3️⃣ Tomato Leaf Curl Virus

Cause: Virus transmitted by whiteflies.

Symptoms: Curling and yellowing of leaves, reduced yield.

Prevention: Remove infected plants, use insect nets, and resistant varieties.
